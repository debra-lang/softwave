Against the current UI: the current site communicates the same things with bordered cards, one accent and a small reactive background. The hybrid adds (1) a living sound field and a typographic hero, (2) an orb player with per-sound personality, (3) sound objects instead of letters in Discovery, (4) the Lab as a darker studio with a featured Discovery panel, (5) a painterly frequency canvas, (6) a sound fingerprint for the profile, and (7) cards without hard borders — the same functionality, a recognisably Softwave look.
