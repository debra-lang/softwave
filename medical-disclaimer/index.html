Medical disclaimer

Last reviewed 22 August 2026

This app is designed for sound masking, relaxation, and tinnitus management support. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure tinnitus and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Nothing on this site is medical advice. Experimental features are exploratory tools for relaxation, attention, sound personalisation and tinnitus management support; they are not proven treatments and may not work for everyone. The frequency and matching tools are not hearing tests.

When to talk to a professional

See a doctor, audiologist or ENT specialist if your tinnitus is new or changed suddenly; is persistent (around six months or more) or distressing; is in one ear only; comes with hearing difficulty, fullness or dizziness; pulses with your heartbeat; started after an injury; or is affecting sleep, mood or concentration. A hearing evaluation is the usual first step.

Softwave has no medical staff and makes no claims of clinical review. Sources used for guidance are listed on Research & Sources.
