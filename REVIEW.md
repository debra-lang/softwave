# Softwave — product review before the "personal sound discovery" upgrade (22 Aug 2026)

## What already works well (keep unchanged)
- Choose sound → play in seconds; presets; 14 seamless synthesised sounds; soft defaults, fades, limiter.
- Mixer (5 layers, balance, saved mixes), Frequency Generator, Find My Tinnitus Sound wizard, Sleep Mode with timer + fade, dark sleep screen.
- Visual Focus library (32 visuals), Focus Mode with auto-hiding controls, Movement levels + Reduce Motion, saved combinations.
- Learn section and the static Learn/tool pages with sources and dates; disclaimers; privacy stance (local-only).
- Mobile layout, PWA/offline, SEO pages, light/dark themes.

## What felt unfinished or duplicated
- **The Lab had 29 experiments**, many overlapping: A/B Test vs Sound Discovery; Living Sound vs Recipes' "organic" modifier vs Generative; Moving Sound vs Sound Space drift; Attention Target vs Attention Activities vs Touch; Adaptive Journey vs Sleep Journey vs Timeline; Explorer vs Find My Tinnitus Sound; Surprise Me vs Build My Session. Several were thin (Near-Silence, Minimal Sound, Eyes-Closed) or had weak evidence (Notched, Modulated Tone).
- **Two "find my sound" ideas were not distinguished**: Find My Tinnitus Sound (what does my tinnitus sound like?) vs discovering which masking sound I prefer.
- Sound Discovery only varied noise colour / nature / stability — not brightness, warmth, width, texture, modulation.
- Custom sounds (sculpted, painted, discovered) could not be saved as *sounds* and reused in presets, mixes or favourites.
- Profile was a list of tendencies with no actions attached.
- Safe-listening copy repeated a "60% volume" rule that has no basis (device % ≠ dB).
- All Lab code loaded on every page view.

## What was simplified
- Lab reduced to **10 experiments in 4 groups** (Discover / Explore / Focus / Sessions); the merged capabilities live inside them (e.g. Attention Focus = follow the light / bubble / ripple / notice the change; Adaptive Journey = stable/gentle/dynamic + optional sleep fade; Sound Space = positions + optional very slow movement).
- Explorer's octave check moved into Find My Tinnitus Sound; the Lab no longer has a second pitch tool.
- Dropped: Notched Sound, Modulated Tone (limited/mixed evidence — still documented in RESEARCH.md), Minimal Sound, Near-Silence, Eyes-Closed, Mixing Point Finder (its idea survives as the "start quiet, raise until it blends" guidance), Sound Recipes, Timeline, Environments (their mixes became presets inside Build My Session), Surprise Me (became "Surprise me" inside Build My Session).

## What differentiates the product now
- A signature **Help Me Find My Sound** A/B discovery that shapes a sound in 9 dimensions, with instant crossfade switching and a preference profile that drives real actions (create a sound, build a sleep session, pick a visual).
- Custom sounds as first-class objects: saved, named, playable from the homepage, usable in mixes, favourites and sleep.
- Sound + visual journeys that evolve together.
- Honest evidence labels and research transparency; free tier stays complete; premium is framed as personalisation.
