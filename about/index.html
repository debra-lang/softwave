About Softwave

Last reviewed 22 August 2026

Softwave is a free web app for people with tinnitus who want comfortable background sound. It is built around one idea: everyone experiences tinnitus differently, so everyone’s comfortable sound is different too. Softwave makes trying, comparing and shaping sounds easy, keeps every level low, and helps you keep what you find.

What it is

  • A sound generator with fourteen synthesised sounds that loop seamlessly.
  • A mixer, a frequency generator, a guided sound-matching tool and Sleep Mode.
  • Visual Focus — calm visuals to watch while listening.
  • The Lab — personal sound discovery: compare sounds two at a time, paint or sculpt your own, and build sessions; each experiment carries a plain-English "why are we testing this?" note and an evidence label.

What it is not

It is not a medical device, a hearing test or a treatment. It does not diagnose, treat or cure tinnitus. See the medical disclaimer.

How it is made

Everything is generated on your device with the Web Audio API and HTML Canvas. There are no audio files to download and no server; your favourites and settings stay in your browser. The source is open at github.com/debra-lang/softwave.

Independence

Softwave does not sell hearing aids, supplements, courses or "neuromodulation" programmes, and it has no affiliate links. Guidance on this site is written from the sources listed on Research & Sources; where evidence is limited, the page says so.
